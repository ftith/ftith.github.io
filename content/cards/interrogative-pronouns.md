| Kana | Romaji | Explanation | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| なに | nani | Asks what. 何 is read なに in the first example and なん in the second; its reading depends on the word or sound that follows. | <span style="color:red">何</span>を食べますか。<br><span style="color:red">なに</span>をたべますか。<br>What will you eat?<br><br>これは<span style="color:red">何</span>ですか。<br>これは<span style="color:red">なん</span>ですか。<br>What is this?<br><br><span style="color:red">何歳</span>ですか。<br><span style="color:red">なんさい</span>ですか。<br>How old are you? |
| だれ | dare | Asks who. 誰 is a common kanji spelling. | <span style="color:red">誰</span>が来ますか。<br><span style="color:red">だれ</span>がきますか。<br>Who is coming? |
| どこ | doko | Asks where. | トイレは<span style="color:red">どこ</span>ですか。<br>といれは<span style="color:red">どこ</span>ですか。<br>Where is the restroom? |
| いつ | itsu | Asks when. | <span style="color:red">いつ</span>日本へ行きますか。<br><span style="color:red">いつ</span>にほんへいきますか。<br>When will you go to Japan? |
| いくつ | ikutsu | Asks how many or how old, depending on context. | りんごを<span style="color:red">いくつ</span>買いますか。<br>りんごを<span style="color:red">いくつ</span>かいますか。<br>How many apples will you buy?<br><br><span style="color:red">おいくつ</span>ですか。<br>How old are you? (polite)<br> |
| いくら | ikura | Asks how much something costs. | この本は<span style="color:red">いくら</span>ですか。<br>このほんは<span style="color:red">いくら</span>ですか。<br>How much is this book? |
| 何時 | nanji | Asks what time it is or when something happens. | 今<span style="color:red">何時</span>ですか。<br>いま<span style="color:red">なんじ</span>ですか。<br>What time is it now? |
| どう | dou | Asks how or what something is like. | 日本の生活は<span style="color:red">どう</span>ですか。<br>にほんのせいかつは<span style="color:red">どう</span>ですか。<br>How is life in Japan? |
| どうして | doushite | Asks why; なぜ is another, somewhat more formal, option. | <span style="color:red">どうして</span>遅れましたか。<br><span style="color:red">どうして</span>おくれましたか。<br>Why were you late? |
| どんな | donna | Asks “what kind of” and comes before a noun. | <span style="color:red">どんな</span>音楽が好きですか。<br><span style="color:red">どんな</span>おんがくがすきですか。<br>What kind of music do you like? |
| どれ | dore | Asks which one among multiple things. | <span style="color:red">どれ</span>があなたのかばんですか。<br><span style="color:red">どれ</span>があなたのかばんですか。<br>Which one is your bag? |
| どの | dono | Means “which” before a noun; unlike どれ, it must be followed by a noun. | <span style="color:red">どの</span>本を読みますか。<br><span style="color:red">どの</span>ほんをよみますか。<br>Which book will you read? |
| どちら | dochira | A polite way to ask which one or which direction; can also politely ask where someone is from. | 駅は<span style="color:red">どちら</span>ですか。<br>えきは<span style="color:red">どちら</span>ですか。<br>Which way is the station? |
